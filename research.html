Current Projects

Masters Thesis - A GAIA search for companions behind exo-Neptunes migration

Observatoire de Genève · Prof. Vincent Bourrier · Dr. Joao Faria · Jun. 2026 – Present

Preparing two GAIA DR4 studies to detect and characterize the outer companions thought to drive the high-eccentricity migration of close-in exo-Neptunes across the Desert, Ridge, and Savanna. Work includes compiling a homogeneous catalogue of ~400 Neptunian systems, curating radial velocity and imaging datasets to combine with GAIA astrometry, and building the analysis tools to constrain companion masses, orbits, and 3D system architectures within the ATREIDES collaboration.

Distortions in the Stellar Surface

Observatoire de Genève · Prof. Vincent Bourrier · Dr. Adrien Deline · Jun. 2025 – Present

Updating the ANTARESS workflow (Bourrier et al. 2024) to process high-resolution spectroscopy datasets. Work includes deriving a correction term for the stellar surface radial velocity equation to account for oblateness and tidal bulging, and modeling gravity darkening due to tides induced by a companion planet. Updating ANTARESS plotting routines to visualize the effects of stellar surface distortions due to tides induced by a companion planet. The goal of this project is to improve the accuracy of radial velocity measurements and enhance our understanding of stellar surface dynamics.

TESS Search for Transiting Circumbinary Planets

Tufts Dept. of Physics & Astronomy · Prof. David Martin · Sept. 2024 – Present

Modified the circumbinary planet search algorithm STANLEY (Martin et al. 2021) to search TESS light curves for transiting planets. Repurposed the detrending method for TESS cadence and employed linear interpolation on the period vs. theta search grid, cutting compute time in half. Paper in prep for MNRAS (2026).

Identifying high-z galaxies with contrastive learning

Observatoire de Genève · Prof. Pascal Oesch · Romain Meyer · Feb. 2026 – Jul. 2026

Applied contrastive self-supervised learning to JWST NIRSpec/prism spectra from the FRESCO survey to identify high-redshift galaxy candidates. A convolutional neural network is trained to learn spectral representations without redshift labels, whose embeddings are then clustered and compared to rare sources to find other rare candidates hidden in JWST archival data.

Modeling Circumstellar Disk Truncation in Binary Systems

Observatoire de Genève · Prof. Julia Venturini · Arianna Nigioni · Sept. 2025 – Dec. 2025

Derived an numerical function mapping binary eccentricity, separation, disk viscosity, and stellar masses to the truncation radii of protoplanetary disks. Analyzed planet embryo evolution using the Bern Model (Emsenhuber et al. 2021), examining mass growth, orbital migration, and pebble accretion under varying binary parameters.

Direct Dark Matter Detection — XENONnT

Weizmann Institute of Science · Ran Budnik's Lab · Jun. 2023 – Aug. 2023

Worked within the XENONnT international collaboration on direct dark matter detection. Calibrated a neutron generator and designed an experiment to characterize its flux at varying distances and angles. Analyzed experimental data in Python using statistical tests to validate detector signals.
